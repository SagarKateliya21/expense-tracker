Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided at registration.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic logs such as page visits and error reports, used solely for debugging and improving the Service.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data from your device beyond what you explicitly enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve Spendly:

  • Displaying your expenses, summaries, and spending breakdowns within the app.
  • Authenticating you when you sign in.
  • Diagnosing errors and improving Service reliability.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Service. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own commercial use.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in India. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls.

You may request a copy of your data or ask for permanent deletion at any time by contacting us (see section 5). Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Hosting provider — our servers run on a cloud platform that processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Google Fonts — font files are loaded from Google's CDN; Google may log your IP address in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Beyond the above, we do not integrate analytics tools, advertising networks, or social media trackers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to access your data, or want to request deletion of your account, please contact us at support@spendly.app.

We will respond to all privacy-related requests within 7 business days.
